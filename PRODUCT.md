# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Players of narrative police-management games. Desktop first, playable on touch screens.

## Product Purpose
A complete seven-shift original crime story: dispatch officers, investigate a port conspiracy and earn $40,000 for the chief's witness-relocation fund.

## Brand Personality
Bleak, human, restrained. Original 1991 waterfront noir inspired by angular illustrated strategy games.

## Design Principles
Choices carry consequences. Show time, resources and risk before commitment. Let the city dominate the screen. Dialogue in English, subtitles and interface in Russian.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard controls, explicit pause, independent volume controls, persistent subtitles, reduced-motion support. No sound-only information.
