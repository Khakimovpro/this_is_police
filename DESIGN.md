# LOW WATER visual direction

A player sits at a late-night dispatch desk, watching a cold port city. Coal-black furniture frames a muted teal city with oxidized copper highlights, ivory report sheets, and yellow radio markers. This is a illustrated strategy game, with a diegetic city map as the focal point.

Palette: ink #151c20, surface #202b2e, teal #567577, copper #cf7958, paper #e8e0ca, yellow #e5ba66. Angular cut-paper illustrations and hard silhouettes. Original vector artwork only. Condensed headings, monospaced identifiers, legible system body type. Minimal rounding. Screen consists of header, left dispatch list, expansive isometric map, officer strip and contextual dossier. Mobile stacks these surfaces. Motion: slow camera drift in film, brief state transitions in game; obey reduced-motion.
